TRAIN: every 10 minutes a train pulls into the station on the rail line (marked T on the minimap). Step onto the platform and hold it for 60 seconds: that is what calls the horde, a huge wave. If you are still standing there when the bar fills, the train carries you across the map and you evac. Miss the 60 second window and it leaves without you. Stay off the tracks while it arrives. Reward for escaping by train: $5000, the Train hat, and the M-77 Mobile Bunker (the armoured rig you can see chained to the back of the train). It cannot be found or bought anywhere else.

HELICOPTER: each 10-minute slot rolls 50/50 between the train and a helicopter evac (never both). The landing zone is marked H on the minimap. Stand on foot in the zone and stay there for a full 45 seconds (leaving the zone resets the timer) (a slightly smaller horde than the train). Reward: $2000 and the R-9 Sonar Radio Truck: a camo box truck with a giant sonar dish. While you ride in it, a pulse every 10 seconds marks every zombie and hostile on the minimap as small red dots.

Tank: click fires the 105mm HE cannon (285 damage, slow reload) · hold Space (MG button on mobile) for the machine gun · [ and ] resize the minimap (or drag its corner handle)
Military: soldiers at military bases and on patrol are hostile to EVERYONE: you, survivors, raiders and zombies. Tanks and helicopters are only found at military bases. APCs can turn up anywhere.
Barricades: use one from your bag or hotbar, aim with the mouse, scroll or , . rotates it freely (Shift = fine), click places, right-click cancels, E picks one up. 50 hp each, 10 max down at once: zombies chew through them, bullets fly over
Esc pause · Tab inventory · 1-5 hotbar · Q medkit · F eat or drink
In the inventory: click to use or equip, Shift+click to drop, Ctrl/Cmd+click to bind to the hotbar.

Detection: zombies notice you by noise (the faint ring around you) and by sight (the flashlight and moving in front of them make it worse). Sneak, kill the light, and use melee to stay quiet. Gunshots and engines carry far.
Vehicles differ in durability, top speed, acceleration and handling. Cars under 25% health stall until repaired. Hold a green zone for 4s to extract.
